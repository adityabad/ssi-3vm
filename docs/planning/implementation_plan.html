2. Key Technical Solutions & Startup Innovations

A. Account Abstraction & Seamless User Onboarding (Hiding MetaMask)

Problem: Non-technical users struggle with browser wallets, seed phrases, and crypto gas fees.

Solution:

  • Passkeys & Web3Auth: Enable biometric FaceID/TouchID or social logins (Google, Apple ID, Email OTP).
  • Non-Custodial MPC Keys: Private keys are sharded securely using Multi-Party Computation.
  • Under-the-Hood DID: System automatically provisions did:ethr or did:jwk addresses behind the scenes without user popups.

B. Proactive vs. Reactive Flow Architecture

  • Proactive Issuance (Push): When a student graduates, the University IDP triggers a webhook to issuer-service. The VC is pushed directly to the Holder's DIDComm queue via push notifications (FCM/APNS).
  • Proactive Verification Requests: Recruiters send batch credential requests to candidate queues with one-click mobile approval.

C. Advanced Verifier System (Bulk Verification & Data Modes)

  1. Bulk Verification Engine: Batch upload endpoint for processing hundreds of VPs in parallel with Redis caching.
  2. Ephemeral ZK / Non-Storage Mode: Signature and revocation checks executed purely in-memory. Zero candidate PII stored (GDPR / DPDP compliant).
  3. Stored Audit Mode: Cryptographic verification audit trails stored in PostgreSQL for regulatory audits.

D. Native Wallet Pass Export (Google Wallet & Apple Wallet)

  • vc-to-pass Service: Converts W3C VCs into Google Wallet objects and Apple .pkpass bundles with embedded QR codes.
  • Email Dispatch: Automated emails sent to students with "Add to Google Wallet" and "Add to Apple Wallet" direct buttons.

E. Multi-Tenant Enterprise Architecture & SSO

  • Tenant Isolation: Multi-tenant database schema with tenant_id separation and custom institutional subdomains (mit.verifiable.id).
  • Federated Enterprise SSO: Connects University IDPs (Azure AD, Shibboleth, Okta) via SAML 2.0 / OpenID Connect.

Phase 3: Advanced Verification & Bulk Operations (Weeks 7 - 8)
